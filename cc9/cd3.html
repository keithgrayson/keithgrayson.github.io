Cowboys of Cloud 9

The Cowboys return with their latest "Someday Soon" collection of celestial country melodies, featuring Campfire by the Water, Heaven In Paradise, Picking a Song With No Name, She Must Be My Angel, She's So Lovely, The Mute Shall Speak, We Found It, and We'll Watch the Sun Set in Heaven.

The Cowboys have once again crafted a musical journey that speaks to the heart of simple living under infinite skies. From the gentle crackling of a Campfire by the Water where stories are shared beneath starlight, to the transcendent moment when The Mute Shall Speak through the universal language of music, each track tells a story of connection - to nature, to loved ones, and to something greater than ourselves.

Whether it's the tender recognition that She Must Be My Angel or the quiet discovery that We Found It - that elusive peace we all seek - the Cowboys remind us that paradise isn't a distant place but a state of mind found in life's sweetest moments. As they say, the best sunsets aren't just watched, they're felt, especially when We'll Watch the Sun Set in Heaven from our own back porch paradise.
