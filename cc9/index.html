Cowboys of Cloud 9

This is where I share my interest in computer-generated music with friends, and the occasional stranger who wanders in from somewhere else on the web. I've always been drawn to the math underneath music — Fourier transforms, harmonic overtones, the way a waveform breaks down into simple sine components — and to what that structure does to the brain: why a minor third feels wistful, why a steady pulse can lower your heart rate, why some chord progressions make you tear up for no clear reason. The Cowboys of Cloud 9 are my way of playing with those ideas directly. They aren't a band in any human sense — no members, no rehearsals, no green room. They exist only as the flow of energy through silicon, and they've settled, somewhat to my surprise, into a genre of futuristic, synthetic cowboys playing alt country out past the edge of the solar system.

The Cowboys take their music to the frontiers of space with their Memories of Mars series. It features several favorites including 'Drifting Souls', 'Floating on the Marineris River', 'Faces in the Cloud' and the signature track 'Cowboys of Cloud 9'.

Then the Cowboys travel to the back 40 of deep space with their All Electric Intergalactic Outhouse series. It features 'Cowboy Concerto in C', 'Milky Way Breezes', 'Grandpa’s Picking All Night Long', and the all-time favorite 'Sometimes Late at Night (I Hear Angels Singing)'.

The Cowboys look forward with their latest collection of the Someday Soon series. It features 'Campfire by the Water', 'She Must Be My Angel', and 'We'll Watch the Sun Set in Heaven'.

The Cowboys trade the stars for the shoreline with Forever Yours, a collection written closer to home. It features 'Coldwater Creek', 'My Hula Girl', 'Steady as the Rising Tide', and the title track 'Forever Yours'.

And finally an experimental collaboration with AI. The Cowboys play songs created by Claude and Grok. This series of songs are created through the programming skills of Claude combined with the musical skills of Grok. This is still a work in progress, but there are a few sample clips here.
