Cowboys of Cloud 9

This is the home for my interests in computer-generated music. While the Cowboys of Cloud 9 only exist as the flow of energy through silicon, they present the sound of futuristic cowboys in alt country music.

The Cowboys take their music to the frontiers of space with their Memories of Mars series. It features several favorites including 'Drifting Souls', 'Floating on the Marineris River', 'Faces in the Cloud' and the signature track 'Cowboys of Cloud 9'.

Then the Cowboys travel to the back 40 of deep space with their All Electric Intergalactic Outhouse series. It features 'Cowboy Concerto in C', 'Milky Way Breezes', 'Grandpa’s Picking All Night Long', and the all-time favorite 'Sometimes Late at Night (I Hear Angels Singing)'.

The Cowboys look forward with their latest collection of the Someday Soon series. It features 'Campfire by the Water', 'She Must Be My Angel', and 'We'll Watch the Sun Set in Heaven'.

And finally an experimental collaboration with AI. The Cowboys play songs created by Claude and Grok. This series of songs are created through the programming skills of Claude combined with the musical skills of Grok. This is still a work in progress, but there are a few sample clips here.
