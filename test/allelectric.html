Chapter Two · Deep Space

All Electric Intergalactic Outhouse

The Cowboys travel to the back 40 of deep space with their All Electric Intergalactic Outhouse series. It features 'Cowboy Concerto in C', 'Milky Way Breezes', 'Grandpa's Picking All Night Long' and their all time favorite 'Sometimes Late at Night (I Hear Angels Singing)'.

When man first traveled into space, the skeptics asked why we didn't find heaven. The Cowboys answer that question by weaving an instrumental story of 'all electric souls' soaring through the far reaches of the universe, where every soul creates their own heaven. For some, heaven is the ability to explore distant galaxies where space is always blacker just 'over the event horizon'.

But the Cowboys never traveled far from home. They prefer sitting on the back porch feeling the late afternoon Milky Way breezes. For the Cowboys, heaven is the simple pleasures of an 'all electric outhouse' nestled amongst the wonders of God's universe. A simple place in a majestic setting where they spend their days picking their guitars and their nights listening to angels singing.
